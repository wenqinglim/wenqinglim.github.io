## 🧪 Eval resources I used to get started

- [Your AI Product Needs Evals](https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/evals/) by Hamel Husain. **Start here.** Why evals matter, and why looking at your own data comes before any metric.
- [Evaluating the Effectiveness of LLM-Evaluators (aka LLM-as-Judge)](https://eugeneyan.com/writing/llm-evaluators/) by Eugene Yan. **Read this before you build a judge.** A practical survey of what works (and what doesn't) when an LLM does the grading.
- [Who Validates the Validators?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.12272) by Shreya Shankar et al. **The research version of this talk.** How to align LLM judges with human preferences, and why your criteria keep shifting as you look at more outputs.
- [Hex engineering blog](https://hex.tech/blog/category/engineering/). **For evals in practice.** Helpful for thinking about evals in general, and especially for evaluating AI analytics features like the ones we build.
